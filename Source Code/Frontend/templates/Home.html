What is Garbage Classification?

Garbage classification is the process of categorizing waste into biodegradable, recyclable, hazardous, and electronic waste. This ensures better recycling, reduces pollution, and supports sustainable living.

Benefits
  • Promotes recycling and reduces landfill waste
  • Prevents pollution and protects the environment
  • Supports sustainable development
  • Reduces greenhouse gas emissions
Types of Waste
  • Biodegradable – food scraps, garden waste, paper
  • Recyclable – plastics, glass, metals, cardboard
  • Hazardous – batteries, chemicals, medical waste
  • E-Waste – computers, mobiles, electronics
